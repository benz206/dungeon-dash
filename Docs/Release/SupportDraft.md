## Getting started

Choose **Enter the Dungeon**, create a character, and follow the on-screen guide.
Use the left control to move, the right control to aim and attack, and **Dash**
to evade attacks. Orange rings and aim lines warn you where enemies will attack.
Clear every enemy in a chamber, then approach its exit to go deeper.

## My purchase is missing

Open **Styles** from the title screen and select **Restore Purchases** while
connected to the internet. Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. A
pending approval is not a completed purchase. If the store is unavailable,
reconnect and try again. Contact **[support route]** if a completed purchase still
does not appear. Never send your Apple password or payment-card details.

## What does Guild Collection unlock?

It permanently unlocks Ember Crown, Astral Wisp, and Verdant Soul cosmetic auras
for every hero. They do not change damage, health, coins, or loot. Classic is free.

## Where is my progress saved?

Character progress and expedition checkpoints are saved on your device. Continue
reopens a saved expedition paused so you can resume when ready. The game saves
during play and at important events; a sudden termination can lose actions since
the latest checkpoint. There is no implemented cloud backup for gameplay saves.
Deleting the app can remove progress. Restore Purchases does not restore a lost
character save.

## Can I play offline?

Dungeon runs work offline. Buying/restoring cosmetics and using the shared online
market need internet access. New players use local simulated listings until choosing Connect Online. When
the initial online connection fails, the game also falls back to local listings. Gameplay coins cannot be bought for real money.

## Reporting a problem

Send **[support route]** the game version, iPhone model, iOS version, what happened,
and the steps that reproduce it. A screenshot can help if it contains no personal
information. For purchase issues, describe the store message and whether Restore
Purchases completed. Do not send passwords or full payment details.

## Privacy and deletion

Link the completed privacy policy here after backend verification. To request
online deletion, open **Online Data**, choose **Delete Account**, read the warning,
and confirm **Delete Forever**. Your local characters and cosmetics are kept;
active online listings and unclaimed proceeds are removed. If deletion is
unconfirmed, reconnect and select **Retry Deletion**. If it still cannot finish,
copy the support ID and send it to **[support route]** so the account can be checked.
Do not uninstall the app while troubleshooting: its local progress has no cloud
backup. Deleting a character slot is separate from deleting the online account.
